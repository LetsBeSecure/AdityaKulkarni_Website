About

Area of research

My work spans the field of cybersecurity, with two core directions: strengthening web security through phishing webpage detection, and advancing network security via DNS security enhancements and privacy-preserving protocols. Beyond these, I explore AI-driven cybersecurity and cryptography.

Journey

My research journey has been centered on designing solutions that are practical, robust, and resilient against emerging cyber threats. Explore my work and projects to walk through the milestones and contributions that shaped this path.
